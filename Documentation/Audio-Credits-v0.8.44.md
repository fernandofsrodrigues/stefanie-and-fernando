## Motorcycle recording

The motorcycle uses a recorded idle by lmbubec, Motorbike Idling.wav, licensed CC0 1.0. Source: https://freesound.org/people/lmbubec/sounds/119455/ ; license: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . A publicly served high-quality MP3 preview was downloaded, converted to mono, trimmed from 4 to 12 seconds, joined with a 150 ms linear overlap, normalized to peak 0.78 and exported as a 7.85-second 48 kHz PCM16 loop. This is a generic recorded motorcycle, not a recording of either depicted model. The existing six-source vehicle pool is reused; pickup doors and ignition cues are suppressed for bikes. Author/license/source snapshots, original download and transformed WAV are in AudioLicenses/Motorcycle-v0827. Human listening remains pending.
